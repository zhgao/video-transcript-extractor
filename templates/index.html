🤖 AI Technologies

🎙️ OpenAI Whisper

State-of-the-art speech recognition AI that transcribes audio to text with high accuracy. Works on any video with speech, even without captions.

🦙 Llama 3.2 (Ollama)

Advanced language model running locally on your machine. Generates intelligent summaries of transcripts in seconds.

⚡ yt-dlp

Powerful video downloader supporting 1000+ platforms. Extracts audio and metadata from videos efficiently.

✨ Key Features

  • Multi-Platform: YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Twitter, and 1000+ sites
  • Automatic Fallback: Tries fastest method first, falls back to AI if needed
  • Real-Time Progress: See transcription progress as it happens
  • AI Summarization: Get concise summaries with one click
  • Beautiful Formatting: Auto-formatted paragraphs for easy reading
  • Privacy First: All AI processing happens locally on your machine
